Community Guidelines

Welcome

Acetyl Client is meant to be a fun, energetic, and welcoming chat app for everyone. Whether you’re here to chat with friends, join new communities, or explore what this platform can do, these guidelines help keep things safe, respectful, and enjoyable. Think of this page as both a quick beginners guide and an agreement on how we treat each other.

Getting Started

You can jump into the Public server right away or enter a server code to join private spaces. Creating your own server is just as easy—pick a name, and share your invite link or code with others.

Messages support text, emojis, file and image attachments up to 10MB. Feel free to express yourself, but keep conversations readable and avoid flooding channels (aka spamming). You can start a direct message or mention someone by right-clicking their message or typing @username, in the chat box.

The Actual Guidelines

Be Respectful. Treat everyone like a real person. Harassment, hate speech, bullying, discrimination, and NSFW content are strictly forbidden.

Keep Things Safe. Do not share illegal content, dangerous links, malicious files, or anything that compromises user security.

Voice Channel Rules. Keep voice calls friendly and enjoyable. Loud audio spam, soundboards without consent, and disruptive noise are prohibited.

No Spam or Ban Evasion. Avoid message spamming, automated bot activity, or attempting to bypass server mutes, kicks, or platform bans with secondary accounts.

Use Common Sense. If you’re unsure whether something is appropriate, it’s best not to send or upload it.

Safety & Moderation

Admins help keep the platform safe and enjoyable. They can step in when needed, remove harmful content, and issue bans. If you’re banned, run into a user issue, or need help with your account, you can reach out via our Support Page or email support@acetyl.cc to appeal or get support.

If you run into a problem — whether it’s a bug, a user issue, or something that feels off — you can visit acetyl.cc/support and contact us by using one of the options.

Acetyl isn’t intended for use on school‑managed networks or devices. Many schools block chat apps, so please respect your institution’s rules.
